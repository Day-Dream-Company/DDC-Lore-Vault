“I don’t know what you think you’re proving by running away all the time.”

“Getting tired yet?”

“No. Not enough to give up any time soon, anyway.”

“It doesn’t matter how skilled you are if you don’t have the endurance to finish a fight. *Which is why you need to train.*”

[[Aster Kenos|Aster]] rolls his eyes, says “You know what? You’re right. I should have ended this already,” and summons his interface. The faceted sphere hovers in his palm, and as it glows green [[Daz Lastname|Daz]] suddenly feels the crushing weight of gravity.

[[Daz Lastname|Daz]] stumbles coming out of his teleport, landing with one knee on the ground. He disappears again, but reappears nearby under just as much weight. “Hey, no fair,” he says, slowly pushing himself to stand up. “You’re using your [[Relay|relay]]. An enemy wouldn’t have that on them.”

Aster simply said “Excuses, excuses,” and lunged up to hit him.

[[Daz Lastname|Daz]] blinked out of existence again, but this time [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] didn’t even need to accelerate and listen for him to reappear. He knew exactly where his [[Relay|relay]] was at all times, and now that he was paying attention to it there would be no catching him by surprise. He tried teleporting behind [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] once or twice, but with no luck. Each time he reappeared [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] was already nearly facing him, and the weight of his body made him far too sluggish to capitalize on what little temporary advantage there was.

Moving his body cost him more energy than normal, and teleporting forced him to spend more [[Aura]] than normal. Suddenly he was on the same ticking clock he had been giving [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] a hard time about. He blinked once more, this time further away to relative safety behind a tree, and dug into his pockets to retrieve the [[Relay|relay]].

No sooner did he retrieve the small metal and glass object than the world seemed to rotate out from under him. He was dragged sideways out from behind the tree, and then the ground tilted again and he slid, as if falling, towards [[Aster Kenos|Aster]]. [[Daz Lastname|Daz]] threw the [[Relay|relay]] away from himself. It almost felt more like *pushing* himself away from it, rather than simply tossing away a small stone, he separated himself from it nonetheless.

The [[Relay|relay]] continued falling toward aster, bending grass and leaves along with it in its wake, until [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] caught it in his free hand. [[Daz Lastname|Daz]] rolled to his feet, the world having gone back to normal, and said, “Alright I won’t lie, you had me worried there for a second.”

[[Aster Kenos|Aster]] folded both the [[Relay|relay]] and his interface out of existence, but did not drop his aura. “So are we done here?”

“What? No,” [[Daz Lastname|Daz]] laughed. “You cheated, first of all, and you still don’t even get me.”

“You really want me to hit you for real?”

“I still don’t think you can!”

[[Aster Kenos|Aster's]] [[Dynamic|aura]] intensified, and he muttered “Alright fine, have it your way.”

From [[Daz Lastname|Daz’s]] perspective, several things happened simultaneously. [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] simply disappeared, and then before he could process that his vision went black and something heavy hit him *hard* on his lower spine. Hard enough to throw him forward off his feet, but the ground never met him. He was simply falling, floating, in the void. He didn’t feel like he was moving, the air around him felt still. Where *was* he? Was he still even on the same [[Plane]] of existence?

A moment later, before he had a chance to react, his questions were answered, as he crashed back-first into the ground and the world returned. [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] was standing over him, breathing heavily with a clenched fist. His [[Dynamic|aura]] quickly faded, and as he opened his hand a faint wisp of dark smoke wafted up from it.
