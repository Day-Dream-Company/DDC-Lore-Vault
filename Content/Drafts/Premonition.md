Instead of the smooth spherical ethereal surface he expected, he instead saw a much larger dome of flickering geometry and hexagonal facets. Entire regions of segments were missing, and thin, weak panels blinked in and out of existence as they were needed to protect the capital.

This [[Sev'reon]] was dying.

Patches of the hexagonal [[Sev'reon's Dome|dome]] continued to dim and flicker as they struggled to [[Manifestation|manifest]] under reduced power. A dark mass of… *something* crashed against the [[Sev'reon's Dome|barrier]] in the distance, causing the throng of civilians to rebound and scatter like ants in a new direction. [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] felt a thought settle itself gently in his consciousness. A calm, horrifying thought. Just simply, *“I am witnessing the end of the world.”*

A series of lighting columns burrowed straight down through the dark pile of shadowy limbs crawling at the [[Sev'reon's Dome|barrier]], as a streaking shape flew over the swarm on crackling wings. [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] looked up to the floating peak of [[Sev'reon|Sev'reon's]] mountain. At least the [[The Floating Mountain|Mountain's Heart]], the city's [[Aura Reserve|Aura Reservoir]], still glowed and pumped its energy down through the remaining conduits to the ground. Another thought, this time a question: *“What could humanity have possibly done to deserve this…”*

And then, the screaming masses slowly quieted. People stopped moving. They stood still, turned, pointed, held each other, closed their eyes, and refused to believe. High up in the atmosphere, far, far above the fragile [[Sev'reon's Dome|dome]], the dark clouds parted. The smoke and mist were pushed down and away, as what [[Akela Kalani|Akela's]] mind could only tell him was a [[Deity]] fell from the veiled stratosphere. An ancient serpent of infinite wings and eyes, bloodied and broken, fell down into our mortal world. From even higher still, glowing with malice, another [[Ancient Annihilator|impossible being with 8 burning eyes]] looked down on everyone with a hateful joyous reptilian smile. Its claws pierced the sky first, and then its palm pushed several square miles of clouds down toward the city. The size and distance of the thing made the attack appear unbearably slow. Slow enough that you had to wait to die, but fast enough that you'd never outrun its width before death arrived.

As the end of all things bore down on the world, two small lights rose up above the buildings on the far side of the mountain. The wind picked up and began to slowly spiral around them. [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] felt something magical fluctuate nearby. It was something of his, in his pocket. He slipped his hand in to retrieve it, but the second his skin touched its etched metallic surface he remembered.

“[[Aster Kenos|Aster]].”

The two lights. They aren't just lights in the distance, they are people. The green one is [[Aster Kenos|Aster]]. He knows [[Aster Kenos|Aster]], he knows him from the [[Sev’reon Magic School|academy]]. What is he doing? What can any of them hope to accomplish?

Before he could finish his thoughts, his heart took over. [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] lept from his frozen pillar and, with [[Magic]] he did not understand, he took flight. He latched on to the only part of this dying world that he remembered seemed real, and resolved to help his friend, somehow. Then, in his own mind, he heard [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] speak.

The [[Dragon|dragon]] to [[Akela Kalani|Akela's]] right instantly rolled and, with a beat of its scarred wings, thundered off toward the [[The Floating Mountain|floating peak]]. Whoever they were, they must have been able to hear [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] as well. It was a small comfort to [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] that they seemed to know what they were doing. It would be nice to remember what that felt like.

“Be ready to put the [[Sev'reon's Dome|barrier]] back up when I'm gone. I just don't want to damage what we've got left when we do this.”

When he's gone? What is he talking about?

Moments later, the hum of the city collapsed and the [[Sev'reon's Dome|shield dome]] faded into nothing. The silence was eerie, and the sounds of crumbling stone, fires crackling, and the feet of hundreds of people shuffling suddenly stuck out in stark relief. The low, frightened murmuring was again audible as people tried to make peace with their end.

[[Akela Kalani|Akela]] could see [[Aster Kenos|Aster's]] silhouette now. His [[Aura]] was burning brighter than he had ever seen. It wasn't even green anymore. It was white, and he was haloed from behind with that impossible intense light as he levitated in open space alongside his stormy friend. He held up a hand and pointed at the hovering [[The Floating Mountain|Mountain's Heart]], calling down an intense beam of raw [[Aura]] that he captured in his own [[Dynamic|Dynamic's]] refraction.

[[Akela Kalani|Akela]] did not understand what had happened to the capital. He did not understand how his friend had become so strong. The machinery and technology of the city was foreign to him. He did not understand how energy could be projected like this, or what [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] was actually doing, but he could feel the horror and amazement welling up in his gut over what it would no doubt cost him.

[[Aster Kenos|Aster]] [[Channeling|channeled]] [[The Floating Mountain|the crystal's]] raw [[Aura]] into functional [[Magic]], projecting it forward into the space before him. The intensity of the beam multiplied. The [[The Floating Mountain|Mountain's Heart]] brightened as its output increased, and the [[The Floating Mountain|the floating moutnain peak's]] slow unnoticeable movements became agitated as it strained against it's remaining tethers. [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] put his other hand out towards his hovering friend. It was hard for [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] to make out details through the storm and the slow arcing strands of lighting that emanated from her, but he could see the unmistakable shape of large golden horns curling and branching back from her temples.

Her wreath of wind and lighting circled her with rising fury, forming first into a vortex, then flattening into a crisp cutting disk of pressure and light. He [[Channeling|channeled]] more and more [[Aura]] from the [[Aura Reserve|Reservoir]], through himself, and ultimately over to his wrathful-looking friend. [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] pieced together what he was doing. The effects of time acceleration were subtle at this distance, but he was likely increasing the amount of time his friend experienced compared to everyone else. He was trying to squeeze as much energy as possible out of the [[The Floating Mountain|Mountain's Heart]] and into her without [[Aura Poisoning|burning]] her out.

[[Akela Kalani|Akela]] knew there was no way [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] was going to survive this. Accelerating himself like he was doing for the other could not protect him from the effects of [[Channeling|channeling]] so much power. It would only increase the load on him even further. There was no clever trick or exploit this time. Already, the damage was evident. The dark haze of [[Magic|magical]] byproducts emanated from him like the negative corona of a dark star. [[Dragon Glass|Crystal]] circuitry tattooed into his skin burned itself into light, and then nothing, strand by strand. Even if [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] could reach him before he [[Aura Poisoning|burned]] out every biological and artificial fiber of his body, he didn't know what could be done. [[Aura Poisoning]] was one of the few things you can't necessarily just use more [[Magic]] to fix.

[[Akela Kalani|Akela]], and probably at least a few others, then heard [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] speak to them in their minds one more time. Just a single, short, somber phrase. [[Aster Kenos|Aster]] was never particularly flashy with his [[Magic]] if he didn't have to be. He didn't name his spells or announce his moves. But this time was different. Maybe he just wanted to see what it was like, to see if he had been missing out. Maybe he thought there was a chance it might actually help, and he decided to pull out every stop, no matter how minor or silly.

“... [[Moment of Eternity]] ... [[Lorentz Cascade]] …”

Pink and white lightning arced straight out from the horned mage in all directions, evaporating the air around her beyond plasma and into pure energy. [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] was already deaf before his brain even registered the shockwave hitting him. A cylinder of lighting, the width of multiple city blocks, simply *appeared* out from her hands fully formed, aimed upwards towards the [[Ancient Annihilator|ancient annihilator's]] descending palm. It was no longer individual bolts of electricity she manifested. The time acceleration and the sheer quantity of [[Channeling|channeled]] Aura was so great that it was just a solid conduit of raw power.

It phased straight through the palm of the [[Ancient Annihilator|world-ender]] and continued tearing up through the rest of its limb. [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] did not know if [[Great Spirit| Greater Spirits]] had anatomy like people did, but it was clear that this was a grievous injury, even for an immortal. Most of its palm was vaporized. Some fingers were separated, and what pieces were left of those digits continued crashing to the ground. The wounded [[Deity]] recoiled slowly in horror and pain, averting a fraction of certain disaster from the city, for at least a few more minutes.

The display of human spite and resourcefulness did not last long though. After a few moments of normal time, the beams both stopped, [[Aster Kenos|Aster's]] [[Aura]] went dark, and he fell to earth along with the [[Celestial|celestial]] carnage.

As he fell, the [[Sev'reon's Dome|shield dome]] flickered back into existence. The city would have audibly hummed back to life as power was restored if [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] still had eardrums to hear it. His world was gone. This was all wrong. It wasn't fair. He didn't understand why he was being made to lose yet another friend.

He silently screamed and reached out toward [[Aster Kenos|Aster's]] weightless blackened body. Wings and talons sprouted from [[Akela Kalani|Akela's]] body, only to forcibly wrap himself up in a cocoon of water, threatening to drown him inside himself. He swam and thrashed and rejected everything. He denied the world he had seen. He rejected his fear even as he felt himself dragged down deeper into the dark of the ocean of his mind. The only thing he allowed himself to feel was anger, and blame. Blame directed at anything even remotely tangible. Blame especially directed at the cold glowing blue eyes that judged him here, this far under the waves.

The eyes observed him for a while, before eventually [[Akela Kalani|Akela]] was ejected from the being's domain, and as he re-entered his new horrid reality he fell roughly into a stranger's arms.
