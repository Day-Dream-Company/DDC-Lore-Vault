# Physical Form
[[Drico]] prefers to take the form of a serpent, but their nature is fluid and they do not necessarily have a true form, both in size and shape. They have also become accustomed to taking [[Humanoid]] form, as they have found people are particularly interesting to interact with. As they have grown in power, and in knowledge, their physical forms have taken on aspects of stars and nebula, held within translucent skin.
# [[Dynamic]]
Their [[Dynamic]] shares the same celestial aesthetic as their body, because as they grow in power they become less of a physical being and more of a true [[Spirit]] [[Manifestation]]. At a certain level of power they might eventually transcend having a body at all, but they are not yet that powerful.
# Quest For Truth
[[Drico|Drico's]] ultimate goal is to understand the absolute truth of reality, both in terms of physics and rules but also in intent and purpose. One of the ways they attempt to do this is using [[Summoning]] to aquire information. "[[Summoning]]" truth as a concept is technically possible in an abstract sense, but given that [[Summoning]] is limitted by your understanding of the target, it is difficult to aquire information without already knowing that information, and failing to divine that information does not necessarily confirm to you whether it exists or not. It may have failed because the target does not exist, but it also may fail because you are simply not strong enough to complete the magic.

Given this limitation of confirmation, the only way to be sure you get the answer is to be the maximum level of magical strength in order to eliminate the possibility of failing to complete the spell. [[Drico]] finds himself perpetually needing to be stronger by any means in order to validate his quest for knowing.
